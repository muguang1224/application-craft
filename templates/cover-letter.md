# Cover letter template (job application)

Keep it under one page. Four paragraphs. Argue fit — don't restate the resume.

---

**[Your name]**
[email] · [phone] · [LinkedIn/GitHub]

[Date]

Dear [Hiring Manager name, or "Hiring Team" if unknown],

**¶1 — The hook (why *this* role, specifically).**
Name the role and one concrete reason you're a fit that isn't obvious from the resume. Reference something real about the company/product/mission.

**¶2 — The proof.**
Pick the single most relevant accomplishment and make it concrete: what you did, the scale, the outcome. One story, with a number if you have one. This is the paragraph they'll remember.

**¶3 — The bridge (address the hardest requirement).**
Take the one requirement you don't fully meet and say what you *do* have that maps to it. Honest, specific, no spin.

**¶4 — The ask.**
One line. "I'd welcome the chance to talk about how I can help with [their goal]." Thank them.

Sincerely,
[Your name]

---

**Anti-patterns to avoid**
- "I am writing to apply for…" — cut it.
- Restating every job on your resume.
- Adjectives with no evidence ("passionate", "hard-working").
- More than one page.
- Generic letters sent to ten companies — the ¶1 hook is what makes it not generic.
