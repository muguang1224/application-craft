# Cover letter to the editor (journal submission)

Concise, factual, professional. One page or less. Nothing here replaces the manuscript — editors read it to route and triage.

---

[Date]

Dear Editor,

**Re: Submission of "[Manuscript title]" for consideration in *[Journal name]* ([Article type])**

**¶1 — What and why here.**
State the title, article type, and why this journal is the right venue (scope fit, readership). One or two sentences.

**¶2 — The contribution.**
What is new? State the question, the approach, and the principal finding in plain terms. This is the part an editor uses to decide whether to send it out.

**¶3 — Fit and integrity.**
- Confirm it is original, not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approved submission.
- Note any prior dissemination (preprint server, conference abstract) — journals often require this.
- Suggest 3–5 potential reviewers and note any conflicts (many journals ask for this).

**¶4 — Required statements.**
Point to the manuscript's included statements: data availability, funding, competing interests, ethics/consent approvals. (See `../references/journal-submission-checklist.md`.)

Thank you for considering the manuscript. I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,
[Corresponding author, affiliation, email]

---

**Do** check the journal's current author guidelines first — required cover-letter contents and reviewer-suggestion rules differ by journal.
